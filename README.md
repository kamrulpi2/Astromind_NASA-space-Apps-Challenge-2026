# 🚀 ASTROMIND: NASA Space Hardware Explorer

![Project Banner](https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400?text=ASTROMIND+Interactive+Space+Game)

**ASTROMIND** is an interactive educational game designed to introduce school-age students to NASA’s hardware left across the solar system. Through the game, users can explore different NASA missions involving rovers, landers, instruments, and probes on the Moon, Mars, and in deep space. 

Instead of simply reading information, students can learn by exploring the missions, completing interactive challenges, and answering quizzes. The project combines storytelling, visuals, scientific information, and gameplay to make space exploration more engaging and easier to understand.

---

## 🎮 Project Demonstration

Our main goal is to show how NASA’s hardware has helped scientists learn more about our solar system while creating an enjoyable learning experience for young space enthusiasts. Each mission presents:
*   The purpose of the hardware.
*   The scientific tasks it performed.
*   The discoveries it helped make.
*   Its current status.

## 💡 How We Addressed the Challenge

We developed an interactive educational game that tells the story of NASA’s hardware across the solar system in a simple and engaging way. We focused on rovers, landers, instruments, and probes located on the Moon, Mars, and in deep space. Instead of providing complex technical information, we converted the data into easy-to-understand visuals, missions, and gameplay so that school-age students can explore and learn while playing. This approach makes space exploration more interesting, interactive, and accessible.

## 🛠️ How We Developed This Project

1.  **Research & Data Collection:** We researched NASA’s missions and the hardware left across the solar system, including the Apollo Lunar Modules, rovers like Sojourner and Opportunity, and probes like Voyager 1 & 2.
2.  **Storylining:** We organized the information about each mission, its purpose, scientific contribution, and current status into a simple storyline.
3.  **Prototyping:** We designed an interactive game interface combining space visuals, storytelling, missions, and educational challenges.
4.  **Integration & Testing:** Finally, we integrated the collected NASA data with the game environment and tested the overall experience to ensure smooth and educational gameplay.

## 🎯 What We Hope to Achieve

Through this project, we hope to make space science more interesting, accessible, and enjoyable for school-age students. We aim to:
*   Help students understand NASA’s space hardware and scientific discoveries through an engaging game rather than traditional methods.
*   Encourage curiosity about space exploration.
*   Inspire students to learn more about STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics).
*   Show how NASA’s missions have expanded our understanding of the solar system.

## ✨ Highlighted Features

*   **Interactive Simulations:** Navigate and explore the lunar surface and Martian terrain.
*   **Real NASA Data Integration:** Accurate historical and scientific facts.
*   **Engaging Mission Narratives:** Story-driven levels (e.g., Level 1: Lunar Roving Vehicle, Level 2: Sojourner).
*   **User-Friendly Interface:** Built specifically for school-age children.
*   **Interactive Quizzes & Challenges:** Test knowledge after exploring a mission.
*   **Mission Status & Discoveries:** Up-to-date tracking of active and retired space hardware.
